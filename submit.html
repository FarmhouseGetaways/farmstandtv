For farm stand owners

Get on the map.

Submit your information and we will add your farm stand to the official Ramona Farmstand Map — the printed one, the Google Maps version people navigate with, and the Google Earth version. It is free, and there is nothing to sign up for.

Everything marked listed appears publicly on the map. Your name does not.

We read every one and we visit before we list. The address is what places your pin, so please make it the one a car should drive to — not a mailing address.

What happens next
  1. We read it. Every submission comes straight to us.
  2. We come and see you. Nothing goes on the map that we have not stood in front of.
  3. You go on all three maps — the printed illustration, the Google Maps version people navigate with, and Google Earth.
  4. You may end up in a tour. If it is a good story, Carissa will ask about filming.
