On the day

How the crawl works.

  1. Open the map. You’ll see the stands on this crawl. Every other stand in Ramona is on the main map.
  2. Start the tour. Your phone asks where you are, then shows you on the map. We suggest a driving order from there. To pick your own, drag a stop, use the up and down buttons, or tap the stands in the order you want.
  3. Head to the next stand. You’ll see how far it is and a rough time. Pull in close and that stand gets a stamp, then the next one comes up. Your stamps stay on this phone if you leave and come back, and the screen tries to stay on while you drive.
  4. Get directions. Tap Directions and your phone’s map app (Google Maps or Apple Maps) will guide you turn by turn to the next stand.
