Ramona Farm Stand Christmas Crawl

The Ramona Farm Stand Christmas Crawl is Saturday and Sunday, December 19 and 20, from 9 AM to 5 PM. Visit as many of the 14 farm stands below as you like, in any order. Each one is decorated for the holidays and stocked with local goodies. Tap Start the tour and the map will take you from stand to stand.

How the crawl works.

  1. Open the map.

    This map shows only the stands on the crawl. Every other stand in Ramona is on the main map.

  2. Start the tour.

    Your phone asks where you are, then shows you on the map. The suggested order starts at Mini Barn Market. Press and hold a stop and drag it to change the order.

  3. Go to the next stand.

    You’ll see how far it is and a rough driving time. When you arrive, that stand gets a stamp, then the next one comes up. Your stamps stay on this phone if you leave and come back. The screen tries to stay on while you drive.

  4. Get directions.

    Tap Directions. Your phone’s map (Google Maps or Apple Maps) takes you turn by turn to the next stand.
